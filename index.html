Questions

Good to know before you start.

Do I have to mail you my cards?

No. The service is designed to work from clear photographs and information you provide. You retain possession of your cards.

Do you guarantee card values or sales?

No. Research is informational, comparable sales may be limited, and market prices change. We do not guarantee value, sale price, profit or a sale.

Do you grade or authenticate cards?

No. We do not provide professional grading or authentication. We can flag visible features that may deserve further review.

Will you publish listings on my eBay account?

No. The service prepares draft listing information and inventory files. You review, edit and publish listings yourself.

How long does an order take?

Timing depends on quantity, image quality and research complexity. A delivery estimate is confirmed in writing before payment.
