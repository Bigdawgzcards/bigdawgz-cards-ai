What we need from you

Clear photos

Front and back, good lighting, no glare, card number visible where possible.

Your preferences

Sport, marketplace, preferred spreadsheet fields and whether you want descriptions only or research quoted separately.

Your final review

AI can make mistakes. You verify player, year, set, number, parallel, serial numbering and all listing details.

Frequently asked questions

Do you buy cards?

No. This is a service for customer-owned cards. You keep your inventory.

Do you grade or authenticate?

No. We organize visible details and may describe visible condition indicators, but we do not provide professional grading or authentication.

Can you guarantee card values or sales?

No. Prices and sales depend on the market and the card. Any optional sold-listing research is informational, not a guarantee.

How fast is delivery?

Timing is agreed after we see a sample and confirm the workload. No turnaround is promised until confirmed in writing.
